Electric Range

L1 End-of-Test Speed Threshold
Cycle threshold = 0.85 × maximum driving-cycle speed
Vehicle threshold = 0.85 × maximum vehicle speed
L1 threshold = MIN(cycle threshold, vehicle threshold)

AIS-040 Measurement Accuracy / Resolution Reference

REFERENCE

Measurement Parameters

  • Time: ±0.1 s accuracy / 0.1 s resolution
  • Distance: ±0.1% accuracy / 1 m resolution
  • Temperature: ±1 °C accuracy / 1 °C resolution
  • Speed: ±1% accuracy / 0.2 km/h resolution
  • Mass: ±0.5% accuracy / 1 kg resolution
  • Electricity balance: ±0.5% accuracy / 0.3% resolution

L1 Reference

The L1 end-of-test threshold is calculated as 85% of the lower of the maximum driving-cycle speed and maximum vehicle speed.
