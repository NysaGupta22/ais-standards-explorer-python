Starting & Extrapolated Gradeability

Method of measurement and regulatory requirements for starting gradeability capability (Part 1), and mathematical extrapolation for variant vehicle weights, powertrain torques, transmission ratios, and tire sizes (Part 2).

Starting Gradeability (Bench Test Calculation)

AIS-003 PART 1
G = 100 × tan[ sin−1( sin q × WT / WR ) ]
Starting Gradeability (G) —
Calculates the starting gradeability percentage of the vehicle based on regulatory incline measurements.

Extrapolated Gradeability (Variant Extrapolation)

AIS-003 PART 2
Gn = (Tn / To) × (Grn / Gro) × (GVWo / GVWn) × (ro / rn) × Go
Extrapolated Gradeability (Gn) —
Extrapolates tested gradeability to a new variant configuration with modified powertrain or vehicle design variables.
