AIS-038 FORMULA CALCULATOR HIGH-VOLTAGE ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Traction Battery Insulation Resistance

Evaluation of electrical isolation resistance (Ri) for electric powertrains and rechargeable electrical energy storage systems (REESS). Applies the standard DC voltmeter measurement method using a known test resistor (R0) on either the negative bus (V1) or positive bus (V1′).

Insulation Resistance Measurement Method (AIS-038 / UN R100)

STANDARD TEST PROCEDURE
Condition 1: When V1 ≥ V1′ (Negative Bus Insertion)
Ri = [ (V1 − V2) / V2 ] × R0

If measured voltage between negative HV bus and chassis (V1) is greater than or equal to positive bus (V1′), insert R0 between negative bus and chassis to measure V2.

Condition 2: When V1′ > V1 (Positive Bus Insertion)
Ri = [ (V1′ − V2) / V2 ] × R0

If measured voltage between positive HV bus and chassis (V1′) is greater than negative bus (V1), insert R0 between positive bus and chassis to measure V2.

MINIMUM PASS CRITERIA (AIS-038 MANDATORY):
Rmin = U × 500 Ω/V   (Minimum 500 Ω/V of Nominal Voltage U)
