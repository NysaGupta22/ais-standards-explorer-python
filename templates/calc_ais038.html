AIS-038

Traction Battery Insulation Resistance

AIS-038 covers specific requirements for the electric power train and rechargeable electrical energy storage system (REESS) of applicable vehicles. Electrical isolation is one of the safety areas addressed by the standard.

STANDARD OVERVIEW

What is AIS-038?

AIS-038 is an Automotive Industry Standard addressing safety requirements associated with the electric power train and rechargeable electrical energy storage system (REESS).

The standard includes requirements and test methods relating to electrical safety, protection against hazardous electrical parts, electrical isolation and REESS-related safety.
